CheryEarn

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

CheryEarn respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use CheryEarn and how that information may be used.

2. Information We Collect

When you register or use CheryEarn, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Account and login information
  • Referral information
  • Earnings and transaction information
  • Withdrawal information
  • Information you provide when contacting support

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be used to:

  • Create and manage your CheryEarn account
  • Process eligible payments and withdrawals
  • Track referrals and rewards
  • Maintain account balances and transaction records
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve CheryEarn services
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. Referral Information

CheryEarn may record referral codes, referral relationships and qualifying referral activity so that the referral program can operate correctly.

5. Payments and Withdrawals

Where payment or withdrawal services are used, certain information may be provided to the relevant payment service provider when necessary to process a transaction.

Transaction information may also be retained for account records, security and dispute resolution.

6. Password Security

Your password is intended to protect your account. You should keep your login credentials confidential and should not share them with other people.

If you believe your account has been accessed without your permission, contact CheryEarn support as soon as possible.

7. Information Sharing

CheryEarn does not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the platform, process transactions, provide technical services, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

However, no internet service can guarantee complete security of information.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

CheryEarn may use browser storage or similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember settings and support normal website functionality.

10. Account Information

You should provide accurate information when creating your account and keep your information up to date where necessary.

11. Children's Privacy

CheryEarn is not intended for individuals who are not legally permitted to use the service. Users should only create an account where they are legally eligible to do so.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version may be published on this page together with a revised date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact CheryEarn through the support channels provided on the platform.
