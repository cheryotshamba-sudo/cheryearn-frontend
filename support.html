Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Tap Register, provide your required information, and complete the registration process.

Where can I find my referral link?

Your referral code and referral link are available from your CheryEarn dashboard.

How can I check my earnings?

Open the Earnings section from your dashboard to view your available balance and earning history.

How do I request a withdrawal?

Open the Withdraw section, enter the required amount and M-Pesa number, then submit your request.

What if my withdrawal is delayed?

Check your withdrawal history first. If the issue continues, contact support with your transaction details.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the Forgot Password option on the login page to request a password reset.
